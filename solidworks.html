Taking the initiative

I earned my Certified SOLIDWORKS Associate (CSWA) certification in March 2026 through self-study. I set the goal for myself because I wanted to become more confident in CAD and contribute more effectively to GW Electric Yacht’s design work.

Learning through practice

I studied SOLIDWORKS and worked through practice CAD models to build my understanding of part modeling and assemblies. Repeatedly creating and refining models helped me turn what I was learning into practical skills I could apply to the electric boat’s motor and drivetrain. Preparing for the certification gave me a clear goal and motivated me to keep improving independently.

Working toward the CSWP

I am now actively pursuing the Certified SOLIDWORKS Professional (CSWP) certification. I am continuing to study and practice CAD to deepen my modeling skills and build on the foundation I developed while preparing for the CSWA.
